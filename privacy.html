Privacy Policy

TPP Backup is a personal utility operated by its owner for encrypted backup storage in Google Drive.

Google Drive Access

The application requests access to Google Drive to upload, list, verify, download and delete backup files.

Data Storage

Backup archives are encrypted before being uploaded to Google Drive. OAuth credentials and encryption keys are stored locally by the owner.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell personal information or share backup contents with advertisers.

Data Retention

The automated backup process is designed to retain the latest three completed backup sets.

Data Control

The owner controls the application, its Google Drive authorization, encryption keys and stored backups.
